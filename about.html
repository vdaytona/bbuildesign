Our story

Founded on a simple idea — do it properly.

B&B Building and Design Pty Ltd — previously known as B Yang Construction Pty Ltd — was founded in Sydney by Bruce with one ambition: to deliver buildings that hold up to scrutiny, both today and decades from now. We work closely with homeowners, architects and consultants to plan, document and execute every project with rigour.

From the first conversation we set out a clear scope, a transparent budget, and a programme that respects your life and your home. The work happens behind the scenes — and shows up in every flush detail and quiet finish.

We are based in Concord and work across greater Sydney, from the Eastern Suburbs and Inner West to the North Shore and beyond.
