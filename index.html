About B&B

Crafting homes that endure, with the precision of a design studio and the rigour of a master builder.

For more than a decade, B&B Building and Design has partnered with Sydney homeowners and architects to bring considered, well-detailed projects to life. From the first sketch to the final handover, every decision is made with intention.

We believe great construction is invisible — it is the silent work behind a perfectly aligned joint, a flush detail, a room that simply feels right.
