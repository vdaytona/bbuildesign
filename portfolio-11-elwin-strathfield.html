The residence

Completed in 2026, 11 Elwin Street brings together a pale exterior, timber detailing and landscaped outdoor spaces. A curved canopy frames the front entrance, while broad glazing at the rear connects the interiors with the pool and garden.

Details throughout

Veined stone gives the kitchen and bathroom their character, balanced by light timber floors and cabinetry. In the living room, a stone fireplace and long hearth create a focal point. A curved stairwell draws daylight through a circular skylight, and the covered outdoor kitchen sits alongside the pool.

  • Curved entrance canopy and timber garage door
  • Stone kitchen island and matching splashback
  • Stone bathroom vanity with integrated basins
  • Timber staircase beneath a circular skylight
  • Pool, garden and covered outdoor kitchen
