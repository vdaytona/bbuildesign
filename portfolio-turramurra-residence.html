Turramurra Residence

A refined upper-north-shore family home — anchored by a warm shaker kitchen, gold tapware, and a connected living and dining space that quietly invites the day.

LocationTurramurra, NSW
TypeWhole-house Renovation
Year2024
Duration7 months

The brief

A young family had outgrown the closed, dark plan of their existing home. They wanted warm finishes, an open kitchen at the heart of the house, and a living room that worked equally well for quiet evenings and weekend gatherings.

What we did

We opened the plan, reworked the kitchen with cream shaker joinery and gold tapware, and added a skylight that drops natural light into the centre of the home. The living and dining rooms are layered with built-in joinery, a recessed fireplace and warm timber floors throughout.

  • Cream shaker kitchen with timber-clad island
  • Gold gooseneck tapware and slim cabinetry pulls
  • Recessed fireplace and built-in joinery
  • Skylight and engineered timber flooring
The house finally feels like the family that lives in it.
