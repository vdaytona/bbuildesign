North Rocks Kitchen Renovation

A calm, hard-working family kitchen — handleless joinery, full-height marbled stone and a tidy U-shape plan that puts the cook at the centre.

LocationNorth Rocks, NSW
TypeKitchen Renovation
Year2023
Duration6 weeks

The brief

The existing kitchen was small, dated and disconnected from the family's daily routine. The owners wanted a calm workspace — quiet on the eye, generous in storage and easy to keep clean.

What we did

We reorganised the plan into a tight U-shape with a full-height marbled stone splashback, handleless joinery and integrated lighting. The result is a kitchen that does a lot of work without ever shouting about it.

  • Handleless flat-panel joinery in soft white
  • Full-height marbled stone splashback and benches
  • Integrated under-cabinet lighting
  • New gas cooktop, oven and rangehood
It is exactly the calm kitchen we asked for — and a delight to use.
