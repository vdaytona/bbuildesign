A home around the courtyard

At 74 Davidson Avenue, the pool sits at the heart of the home. Broad glazing brings the water and planting into view from the surrounding rooms, while a curved façade and tall arched windows give the street elevation its character.

Inside, a marble kitchen island, custom joinery and pale timber floors create a warm setting for daily life. A marble staircase connects the levels beside the courtyard. Outside, the pool, spa and sunken seating bring a place to gather into the centre of the house.

  • Curved façade and arched windows
  • Central courtyard pool, spa and sunken seating
  • Marble kitchen island and custom joinery
  • Glazed stairwell overlooking the courtyard
  • Five bedrooms and landscaped outdoor spaces
