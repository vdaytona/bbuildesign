Croydon Park New Build

A two-storey inner-west family home with a brick and render façade, brass-detailed bathrooms and a balcony that catches the late sun.

LocationCroydon Park, NSW
TypeNew Build
Year2023
Duration10 months

The brief

A new home for a young family on a long, narrow inner-west block. The brief asked for four bedrooms, two living zones and a low-maintenance street presence — solid, calm and built to last beyond the build cycle.

What we did

We delivered a complete design-and-construct package. A face-brick base grounds the building, with a rendered upper floor and timber-clad balcony adding warmth. Inside, the bathrooms quietly steal the show — hex-mosaic floors, full-tile walls and brass tapware throughout.

  • Face-brick base with rendered upper level
  • Brass-trimmed family and ensuite bathrooms
  • Engineered timber flooring throughout
  • Smart-lock entry and integrated lighting
A home that just works — every room earns its keep.
