46 Brays, Concord

A refined inner-west family residence — organised around a curved poolside living room, a fluted-walnut kitchen and quietly generous bedrooms upstairs.

LocationConcord, NSW
TypeNew Build
Year2024
Duration13 months

The brief

A confident family home for a long, north-facing block — soft on the street, generous and pool-facing at the rear. The clients wanted natural materials, a kitchen with real presence, and a primary suite that felt like a quiet retreat from the rest of the house.

What we did

We built the home from slab to handover, working closely with the architect through the curved-glazing detail, the fluted-walnut joinery and the bespoke marble island. The result is a calm, layered house that opens itself to light and water without ever overstating it.

  • Curved frameless glazing to the pool elevation
  • Fluted-walnut and marble island kitchen
  • Herringbone timber flooring throughout
  • Primary suite with private balcony and ensuite
Every room is a pleasure to walk into — and just as good to leave from.
